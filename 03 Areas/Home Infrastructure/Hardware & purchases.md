## To buy
- [ ] Cabinet (78 × 34 × 195 cm, cane doors) — off-the-shelf or custom
- [ ] Powered USB–SATA adapter or dock (3.5" needs 12 V — bus power is not enough)
- [ ] IEC power strip (C14 plug, UK sockets) for the UPS
- [ ] Short patch cables, braided sleeve, velcro ties, edge grommet trim
- [ ] Anti-tip strap for the cabinet

## Principles
Buy once, prefer repairable and standards-based, avoid vendor lock-in and
subscription-dependent hardware.
